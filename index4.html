About Us

Hey there! My name is Robert, and I have been a resident of Muskogee County for nearly a decade. In 2020, my family and I decided to open our backyard farm to the market to help provide quality food items to the community during a time of need. Since then, we have struggled and grown in many ways and are now ready to take our business to the next level. Marketing is such a crucial part of success in business, but it can be incredibly costly. That’s why The Muskogee Local offers small businesses the opportunity to market themselves to the community in a big way, but at a significantly lower cost. By teaming up with 17 local, non-competing businesses, marketing initiatives become possible for a ridiculously low cost. This is our opportunity to grow together! I look forward to working with you. Please feel free to reach out with any questions.
